Mission & Architecture

About SIGNIFY

Bridging communication divides with respectful, verified Indian Sign Language technology.

Why SIGNIFY Was Created

Over 18 million individuals in India have hearing loss or utilize Indian Sign Language (ISL) as their primary natural language. SIGNIFY addresses critical everyday barriers in healthcare, transportation, classrooms, and social spaces where hearing non-signers and Deaf signers interact.

Continuous Playback Engine

Traditional tools force users to click isolated signs with annoying blank screens between gestures. SIGNIFY introduces a dual-buffering continuous animation pipeline that preloads upcoming signs, synchronizes timeline playback, and enforces authentic ISL grammar structure.

Scope & Future Evolution

SIGNIFY uses a structured verified lexicon rather than uncontrolled automatic guessing. Future versions will integrate computer-vision two-way sign recognition (ISL camera back to spoken audio) and expand regional dialectical variations across Indian states.

Linguistic & Cultural Respect: Indian Sign Language (ISL) is a rich, autonomous natural language with complex morphology, spatial syntax, and cultural heritage standardized through the Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC). Members of the Deaf community are visual-spatial linguistic peers, not individuals lacking capability.
